AI Engineering is the emerging discipline of building scalable, reliable, and performant systems powered by Artificial Intelligence models. While Machine Learning Researchers focus on designing new neural network architectures, AI Engineers focus on applying those models to solve real-world problems.

## Why AI Engineers Care

As the field shifts from "training models from scratch" to "composing pre-trained foundation models," the bottleneck is no longer pure math—it's software engineering. AI Engineers are tasked with:
- Managing the lifecycle of models in production (MLOps).
- Designing Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) systems.
- Optimizing inference latency and costs.
- Evaluating model outputs against human preferences.

## The Intersection of SWE and ML

You do not need a PhD to be an AI Engineer. In fact, traditional Software Engineering (SWE) skills are more important than ever. Concepts like version control, CI/CD, testing, and distributed systems are the backbone of robust AI applications.

### Deep Dive: The Shift in Abstraction
Previously, building an NLP application meant training an LSTM or RNN from scratch on a custom dataset. Today, building an NLP application often means calling an LLM API (like GPT-4 or Claude 3.5 Sonnet) and orchestrating the context window using vector databases.

This shift in abstraction layer is what created the AI Engineer role.
